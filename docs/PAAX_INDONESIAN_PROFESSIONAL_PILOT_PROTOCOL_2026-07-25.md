# PAAX Indonesian Professional Pilot Protocol

## Participants
Minimum: estimator/QS, site engineer, structural or MEP engineer, consultant/contractor reviewer, and project administrator.

## Shadow mode
PAAX creates proposals but does not publish or overwrite official quantities. Human output is completed independently, then compared after freeze.

## Project coverage
At least three independent projects: one building with architecture/structure/MEP, one infrastructure project, and one raster/noisy or revision-heavy project.

## Metrics
Object/quantity error, citation validity, unsupported-claim rate, abstention quality, correction burden, time saved, RFI quality, issue lead time, tool calls, latency, cost, and reviewer trust.

## Go/no-go
No-go if critical project isolation, source citation, unit, authority, or safety failures occur. Auto-confirm target is class-specific calibrated precision, not universal 99%.
